About

Voice Ov Yola is a musician and performer from Yola, Adamawa State, in northeastern Nigeria. Online, he also goes by J Boy Alaaji.

His songs move between Afrobeat, Afro-Dancehall and Afro-Hip-Hop, and are sung in Hausa, Fulfulde and English.
